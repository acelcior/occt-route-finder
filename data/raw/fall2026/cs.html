CS

Campus Shuttle

Shuttle service serving all residential communities, parking lots, and key academic buildings: two loops operating around campus

Route Stops & Schedule

Outer Loop: Continuous green-shuttle loops run Mon–Fri 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM with no fixed timetable: use ETA Transit for real-time arrivals. After 9:00 PM, evening Blue Bus service runs on the fixed schedule below (departing Mohawk). Weekend service runs on a fixed schedule.
Important: Buses departing from the Engineering Building do not serve Mohawk, and buses departing from Mohawk do not serve the Engineering Building. These are separate loops: board at the correct stop for your destination.

New this fall: The Inner Loop is a new on-campus route serving 7 stops in a continuous green-shuttle loop around the Brain (inner campus road).
The Inner Loop runs as a continuous loop, traveling around the Brain (inner campus road). It operates Monday–Friday, 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM, with no fixed timetable.

Passenger Notices

Daytime loops have no fixed timetable. Use ETA Transit for live arrivals. Evening Blue Bus (after 9:00 PM), weekend, and Late Nite runs follow the fixed schedules on this page.
